FPInk on-device recognition models

This app bundles the following pinned model files under assets/recognition.
They run locally through ONNX Runtime; nothing is downloaded at runtime.

PP-OCRv6 small text detection (ppocrv6/PP-OCRv6_small_det.onnx)
  Official PaddlePaddle ONNX export, Apache-2.0
  https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_small_det_onnx
  revision 28fe5895c24fd108c19eb3e8479f4ab385fbfc62

PP-OCRv6 medium text recognition (ppocrv6/PP-OCRv6_medium_rec.onnx and
ppocrv6/PP-OCRv6_medium_rec_dict.txt, the dictionary extracted unchanged from
the export's inference.yml)
  Official PaddlePaddle ONNX export, Apache-2.0
  https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_medium_rec_onnx
  revision 50c7eacafc52fa7bcf4194e8cd08e46f8558504b
  Licence text: licenses/PP-OCRv6-models-APACHE-2.0.txt

Kraken PP-OCRv6 medium recognizer (kraken/ppocrv6-medium-recognition.onnx and
kraken/ppocrv6-medium-alphabet.txt), an ONNX export made for FPInk
  Kraken engine: Apache-2.0, https://github.com/mittagessen/kraken
  Model: canonical Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21788410, mirrored at
  https://huggingface.co/small-models-for-glam/kraken-ppocrv6-medium
  revision 01b574f071bf21cff7d7e2a4f38966925dddc30a
  Licence text: licenses/Kraken-APACHE-2.0.txt
